Day 127 | Short Film

 Sean Han's second short film, Day 127 is a Horror-Thriller film that shows a teenager struggling with a series of panic attacks. Constantly chased by his fears and pressures, he has already tried everything he can to escape from his anxiety. When nothing seems to resolve his issue, an unexpected encounter changes the course of his life.

Directed, Written, Filmed, Edited, and Produced by Sean Han.
